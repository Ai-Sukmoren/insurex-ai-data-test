Key findings

All customers · details in the sections below

1 in 82 customers accepts

Overall acceptance is 1.22% (214k offers, 2.6k sales). PA sells more than Life (62% / 38% of sales). With 98.8% rejections, the target is heavily imbalanced.

Active accounts convert 3× better

Half of the customers had no cash inflow in the last 30 days, and they accept at 0.71%, against 2.07% at 50k+ inflow. The gap is almost all PA. Customers 1–12 months on book reach 2.1%.

Younger customers respond best

Customers aged ≤25 accept at 2.0%, falling to ~1.0% after 35. The drop is driven by Life: 0.72% at ≤25 vs 0.24% at 51+.

Product fit follows affluence

PA is the product for Lower Mass and incomes below 30k. Life overtakes PA in Upper Mass (0.68% vs 0.53%), incomes ≥30k and credit-card holders (0.65% vs 0.32%).

Smaller campaigns, better yield

Jun–Sep campaigns contacted about half as many customers but reached ~1.9% acceptance, vs 0.8–1.3% in the big months. More contacts did not bring proportionally more sales.

Recommendations

  1. Score customers before every campaign. Contacting the model's top 20–30% keeps about half to two-thirds of the sales at a fraction of the contact cost.
  2. Match the product to the customer. Offer Life to Upper Mass, income ≥30k, credit-card holders and the young; offer PA to active accounts with cash inflow, men, freelancers and incomes below 30k.
  3. Prioritise active, newer and younger customers: 30-day inflow above 5k, 1–36 months on book, age ≤30, savings 1k–20k.
  4. Contact fewer, better customers in the big months. Jun–Sep converted at almost twice the rate with half the volume.
  5. Fix the data before the next model: repair the 15-day flow fields, the tenure values and the empty rows, and add a customer ID plus the Policy and Payment data to track persistency after the sale.
