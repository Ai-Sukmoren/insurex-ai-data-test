3 · What drives acceptance

Activity, tenure and age separate buyers from rejecters

Response index = group acceptance ÷ average × 100 · the 8 best and 8 worst groups (≥1,000 customers)

Above averageBelow average
  • Best: 1–12 months on book (170), inflow 50k+ (169), age ≤25 (166): about 1.7× the average.
  • Worst: zero savings (37), Government (47), savings 100k+ (49): about half the average or less.
  • Most of the top 8 are about account activity (inflow, tenure), not demographics.
So what: behaviour beats demographics. Use account activity as the first targeting filter.

Limitations & next steps

What to keep in mind, and what comes next

Honest limits of this analysis

Limitations

  • No customer ID: 2,173 duplicate rows cannot be confirmed as real repeats.
  • No year on campaign month, so seasonality and trend cannot be separated.
  • 26% of customers have tenure = 0 (likely unknown); 839 have an impossible tenure.
  • Policy and Payment data were not provided, so there is no view after the sale.
  • Correlation, not causation: the model ranks customers, it does not prove why they buy.

Next steps

  • A/B test model-targeted vs current targeting in the next campaign.
  • Add a product-choice model (PA vs Life) on top of the buy / no-buy score.
  • Validate on a later time period, not only a random hold-out.
  • Join Policy and Payment data to optimise for persistency, not just the first sale.
  • Put the pipeline on a monthly schedule with data-quality alerts.
